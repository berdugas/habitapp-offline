Habitapp · Design Review
An editorial on attention — the weekly review signal
Vol. 1 · 22 May 2026
The problem

The most important moment of the week is whispering.

On the Today screen, "Weekly review available" appears as an 11-pixel line of text — the same size as a footnote, sharing column space with a streak update and competing for attention with a celebratory "All done" pill underneath.

That hierarchy is upside-down. Closing out a day is automatic and frequent; reflecting on a week is rare, deliberate, and the single highest-leverage action a habit-builder takes. The signal should match the stakes.

Three concrete directions follow — pick one.

Why it disappears

  1. Visual weight is wrong. 11px micro text loses to a 48px donut and a filled mint pill in the same row.
  2. No affordance. It's pressable but has no chevron, underline, icon, or fill — nothing reads "tap me."
  3. Two signals, one slot. The "Weekly review available" line and the "All done ✓" pill collide on the same axis. They should collapse.
  4. No spatial separation. The signal lives inside the title block; without a card, divider, or accent strip, the eye skims past.

The move
When a review is due, a tight primary-filled "Weekly review" pill renders above the existing daily pill. Both pills coexist; the green micro-hint is removed.
Pill content
Text + trailing chevron only. The leading book icon from the earlier draft was dropped per user feedback — "just enough to cover the text inside."
Plus
Small attention dot at the top-right of the consistency donut. hitSlop brings the tap target to ~34pt while keeping the pill visually compact.
Trade-off
Adds one extra line to the title column on review-due days. Worth it on a once-per-week basis.
Implementation
~50 lines across GoalContainer.tsx and ConsistencyDonut.tsx. No new components. No animations in v1 — motion polish deferred.
