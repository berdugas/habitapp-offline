# Habitapp Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 28 May 2026**

This policy explains what information Habitapp (the "app") collects when you use it, how that information is used, and the choices you have. The app is built and maintained by Michael Clarin (the "developer", "we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email **aiyo.maker@gmail.com**.

Habitapp is intended for people aged 13 and older. By creating an account you confirm that you are at least 13 years old.

## 1. Information we collect

When you sign up and use Habitapp, the following information is created and stored:

- **Account information.** Your email address and a password you choose. The password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider and is never readable by the developer.
- **Habit and goal data.** The habits, goals, completion records, weekly reviews, and notes that you create inside the app. This data is stored on your device and also on our servers so it can sync across reinstalls and devices.
- **Subscription and trial state.** A record of when your free trial started and ended, and whether your account is currently in trial or paid status. Habitapp does not currently process payments; we only store this status flag on our servers.
- **Usage analytics.** Anonymous events about how the app is used: which screens you open, which buttons you tap, and basic device information (operating system, app version, device type). These events are tied to a randomly generated anonymous device identifier, not to your email. We use PostHog to collect and analyse these events.
- **Crash reports (Android only).** If the app crashes on Android, we automatically send a crash report containing the technical error, a snippet of the code where it happened, and basic device information. We use Sentry to collect these reports. On iOS, the beta is distributed through Expo Go and crash reporting is turned off.

We do **not** collect:

- Your contacts, calendar, photos, location, microphone audio, or any other data outside the app.
- Advertising identifiers. Habitapp shows no advertisements.

## 2. How we use this information

We use the information above only to:

- Provide the service: save your habits, sync across devices, send you reminder notifications you have set up yourself.
- Fix bugs and improve quality, by reviewing crash reports.
- Understand which features people use, so we can prioritise improvements.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

## 3. Third parties that process data for us

Habitapp relies on the following service providers. Each one is contractually bound to handle your data only on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — stores your account, habit data, and trial state. <https://supabase.com/privacy>
- **PostHog** — receives anonymous usage events. <https://posthog.com/privacy>
- **Sentry** — receives crash reports from Android devices. <https://sentry.io/privacy/>
- **Expo** (Expo Application Services) — builds the app and, for iOS beta testers, hosts the JavaScript bundle that runs inside the Expo Go app. <https://expo.dev/privacy>
- **Google Play** — distributes the Android app and handles installation. <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **While your account exists**, we keep your habit data, account information, and trial state for as long as you use Habitapp.
- **Anonymous analytics events** are retained for up to 12 months and then deleted by PostHog according to its retention settings.
- **Crash reports** are retained for up to 90 days by Sentry and then deleted.
- **If you ask us to delete your account**, we remove your account information, habit data, and trial state within 30 days. We will also ask our analytics provider to delete the analytics events tied to your anonymous device identifier, where this is technically possible.

## 5. Your rights

You can:

- **Access your data.** Email us and we will send you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct your data.** You can edit habits, goals, and reviews directly inside the app.
- **Delete your account and all associated data.** Email us with the subject line "Delete my account" from the email address linked to your account. We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.
- **Stop telemetry collection.** Habitapp does not currently offer an in-app toggle to turn off analytics. You can stop all telemetry by uninstalling the app. We plan to add an in-app toggle before the public release.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA, including the right to object to processing and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address above.

## 6. Children's privacy

Habitapp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please email us and we will delete the account and associated data.

## 7. Security

Account passwords are stored as salted hashes by our authentication provider and are not readable by us. Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS. Data at rest in our database is encrypted by Supabase.

No system is perfectly secure. While we follow common industry practices, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. For significant changes (such as adding a new category of data collection, or a new third-party processor), we will also notify you inside the app or by email before the change takes effect.

## 9. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns? Email **aiyo.maker@gmail.com**.
